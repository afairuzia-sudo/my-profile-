PROJECT RECOVERY · TEAM REBUILD

Rebuilding a Trading Platform Team From Zero

THE PROBLEM

Two months before launch, the entire 4-person team on a forex broker project, including the lead architect, walked out one by one.

THE ACTION

Took direct ownership of the execution, brought in a replacement 4-person team from a completed digital wallet project, and rebuilt execution structure and priorities.

THE RESULT

Delivered with only a 1-month extension despite losing 100% of the original team. The client remained satisfied and the project shipped without further team loss.
