01

Trading Platform: The Core Team Left Two Months Before the Deadline

Context: executive role at AHLICODINGDOTCOM

What broke. The platform's lead architect left with two months to go, and the other three team members followed. The client deadline was still live.

What I did. I pulled in the digital wallet team, who had just finished their own project, and confirmed within minutes that they could take over. I flew to their city to brief them on the project structure and what the client expected. Then I called the client myself, told them plainly what had happened, and asked for a two-month extension. They agreed. I then asked the new team whether they could do it in one month, and they said yes.

Result. The platform shipped using only a one-month extension instead of two. The client was satisfied, and everything was realigned.
